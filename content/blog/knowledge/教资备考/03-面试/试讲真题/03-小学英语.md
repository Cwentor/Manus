## 小学英语《Work for old people》

**内容：**

Helen: Hi, Tom. I’m making some planes to work in an old people’s home this summer.Tom: Really? I did that last summer!Helen: Oh, what did they ask you to help out with?Tom: Mm... Things like reading the newspaper to the old people, or just talking to them. They told mestories about the past and how things used to be.Helen: That sounds interesting.Tom: Yeah, a lot of old people are lonely. We should listen to them and care for them.Helen: You’re right. I mean, we’re all going to be old one day, too.

**基本要求：**

- （1）朗读课文；
- （2）讲授完整的阅读流程；
- （3）全英文授课。

## 小学英语《Can I help you?》

**内容：**

Can I help you?I’d like a toy, please.How about this car?Oh, it’s nice. I will take it.

**基本要求：**

- （1）要有板书；
- （2）让学生朗读对话并掌握句型；
- （3）体现师生互动。

## 小学英语《How was your weekend?》

**内容：**

Grandpa: How was your last weekend, Mike?Mike: Great. I saw a movie with my friends. What about you, grandpa?Grandpa: I went fishing. What did your parents do last weekend?Mike: My mother washed many clothes, and my father cleaned the shoes.Grandpa: You should help them.Mike: Yes, grandpa.

**基本要求：**

- （1）要求师范朗读；
- （2）重视口语练习；
- （3）讲解语法。

## 小学英语《We are a happy family》

**内容：**

We have a happy familyI’m Tom. There are 5 people in my family. They are my father, my mother, my sister, my brother and I.My father is a doctor. My mother is a teacher. We are a happy family.

**基本要求：**

## 小学英语《How tall are you?》

**基本要求：**

- （1）试讲时间10 分钟，用英语授课；
- （2）根据教学内容进行教学设计，体现课堂导入、呈现新知、操作方法、拓展运用等教学环节；
- （3）创设教学情境，示范朗读教学内容，适当设置问题帮助学生阅读和理解文本，注意渗透语法功能，体现过程性评价；
- （4）结合教学内容进行板书设计。

## 小学英语《Can you be a friend with me?》

**内容：**

A, E. I, O. U, Can you be a friend with me?U, A, I, O, E, Can I be a friend with you?A, E. I, O. U, You can be a friend with me.U, A, I, O, E, I can be a friend with you.

**基本要求：**

- （1）设计听说课；
- （2）创设情境，用儿童儿歌的韵律来讲授课文；
- （3）讲授词语顺序的同步对课文意义的影响和情感表达的不同；
- （4）设计游戏进行教学；
- （5）全英试讲。
